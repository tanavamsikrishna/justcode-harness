Bapu,

You have given India her awakening, and I have come to you not as a rebel, but as a son who seeks your blessing before walking a different road.

You taught us that truth is our strength. I believe it still. Yet I ask you — is there only one path to truth? When a mother is beaten before her children, will she fold her hands forever, or will she rise?

You believe that ahimsa alone can melt the British heart. I fear they will not listen until they hear the thunder of our unity. Give me blood, and I promise you freedom — not as a threat to you, but as a pledge to the motherland.

Do not mistake my sword for hatred of your path. Your path gave me the courage to believe India could be free. Mine seeks only to hasten that day, so that your India may live.

Keep your fasts, Bapu. I shall keep my march. History will judge us both, but let it never say we did not love her.

Jai Hind,
Subhas
